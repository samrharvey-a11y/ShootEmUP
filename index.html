Calibrate the screen

Point the camera at the projected board. Four numbered X marks are showing near the corners of the screen. Click the centre of each X in the camera view, in order 1 → 2 → 3 → 4.

Screen edges

If your projector cuts off the edges (you can't see all four X marks, or the score is chopped off), pull the edges in until everything is visible. Recalibrate afterwards.

Camera exposure

Lasers are easiest to see when the camera picture is dark. If your webcam allows it, turn exposure down until the board looks dim but the laser dot still glows.
